I'm a Computer Science student who builds at the intersection of machine intelligence and good design.

Most of my time goes into AI and ML — training models, wrestling with data, and figuring out how to turn a research idea into something that actually runs. But I care just as much about how things feel to use, which is why I build the interfaces too.

I work in tight loops: understand the problem, ship the smallest thing that proves it, then sharpen. I'd rather have one project that genuinely works than ten that demo well and break. Right now I'm looking for internships and research collaborations where I can do exactly that on a real team.
